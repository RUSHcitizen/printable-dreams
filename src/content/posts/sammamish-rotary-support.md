On August 26, 2026, Sammamish Rotary provided funding to support Printable Dreams. I am incredibly grateful for their generosity and for their belief in what I am building.

Printable Dreams turns kids' drawings into real, 3D-printed keepsakes so children can see their ideas brought to life. Support from community organizations like Sammamish Rotary helps this mission keep moving forward.

Thank you to Sammamish Rotary for supporting Printable Dreams and believing that every child's imagination matters. Your encouragement means so much to me, and I am excited to keep growing this work and reaching more kids.
