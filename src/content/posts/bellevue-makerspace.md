---
title: "What We Learned at a Bellevue Makerspace"
date: 2026-11-27
excerpt: "We tried 3D printing at a Bellevue makerspace and learned how much time a print can take, especially when it needs a second try."
---

Early on, we tried using a makerspace in Bellevue to 3D print keepsakes for Printable Dreams. We had one hour to print each time we went. There was a good chance a print could fail, and if it did, there was rarely enough time left to start over.

We also couldn't get to the makerspace as often as we wanted. Between the short sessions and the limited chances to print, it didn't work as a regular way to make keepsakes.

That experience helped us understand why having reliable access to a printer matters. We need enough time to finish a print and enough flexibility to try again when something goes wrong.
