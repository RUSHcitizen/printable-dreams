---
title: "How We Connected with Child Life at Seattle Children's"
date: 2026-11-27
excerpt: "Ursula Schwenn welcomed us and helped Printable Dreams connect with kids at Seattle Children's."
---

When we reached out to Seattle Children's, we connected with Ursula Schwenn through Child Life. We shared what Printable Dreams does: kids send us a drawing, and we turn it into a 3D-printed keepsake they can hold.

Ursula was so kind and welcoming. She helped us connect with kids at Seattle Children's, and we've since turned some of their drawings into keepsakes.

Thank you, Ursula, for taking the time to hear about Printable Dreams and helping us reach the kids we started this for.
